How it works

Task check (on your own, no sign-in)

The quickest way into the method. In Task compass you pick one of 48 bank task types or describe your own, set its profile across nine dimensions and see the radar, effect, feasibility status and time horizon at once. In Impact map all 48 task types sit side by side, filterable by function family and switchable between three scenarios.

Start workshop (facilitator)

Choose "Start workshop", put modules into the playlist and create the session. The projector shows a QR code and a session code. You step through the modules: start, the 3-2-1 countdown runs on its own for quiz questions, then reveal and next. A presenter clicker works too (arrow right or page down). In the lobby you can add up to 30 test bots to rehearse the flow and the projector view with a full room.

Join (participants)

Scan the QR code or open the link, enter the session code and an alias, done. You can join at any time, even halfway through. As soon as the facilitator starts a module, the input appears on your phone. Answer with one tap; for words and sliders, remember to press send. If your phone reloads the page, join again with the same code and you keep your points.

Challenge points

After the 3-2-1 countdown, speed counts. A correct answer earns 500 to 1,000 points, the faster the more. Each further correct answer in a row adds a bonus of 100, up to 500. A wrong answer or no answer earns 0 points and ends the streak.

Results

Pulse check, word cloud, joint compass and voting build up live on the projector and can be saved there as images.

Data

The task library is synthetic and illustrative. In a live workshop only aliases and answers are stored, in the session folder of the Firebase database, until the facilitator deletes the session.

Diagnostics

Select the version number at the bottom of the screen to open diagnostics with the phase log and the error log. For support, choose "Copy log" and send the text along.
